# Cycling

I started long distance cycling around 2021.

## Why I cycle

Cycling in nature:

- relaxes the mind
- trains the body
- brings new scenery
- is a way to travel
- builds endurance and patience

## Freiburg

Tours I rode while living in Freiburg:

- **Freiburg to Luxembourg**: a 3-day tour, passing through Metz.
- **Freiburg to Colmar**
- **Freiburg to Mulhouse**
- **Freiburg to Offenburg**
- **Freiburg to Strasbourg**

## Nuremberg

In Nuremberg, I began choosing a starting point and an end point for each ride, and getting to the start first by train or bus.

I record these rides on komoot: [my recorded rides on komoot](https://www.komoot.com/user/1972942628192/activities?type=recorded).

**Recorded since moving to Nuremberg** (as of November 2026): 850 km in 48:50 hours of riding.
