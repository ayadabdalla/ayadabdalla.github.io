We all wants to understand complex concepts in a simple and accesible way. For instance, when I leave my home, I usually have a single number in mind to check that I have all my important items. I check for 4 items in my bag and pockets, the wallet, my phone, my keys, and the gym band. If I added or removed items, I just have to remember that number and then check my pockets.

Did I manage to summarize a concept in a single variable? I'll leave this argument to you.

With this introduction, I would like to hint the reader that what's coming is an introduction into auto-encoders, mathematical mechanisms, that take in complex raw, and arbitrarily large input, and convert them to a decent number of variables, without us choosing what those variables mean.
