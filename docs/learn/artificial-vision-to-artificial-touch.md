# Artificial Vision to Artificial Touch

We like to project our understanding of our own senses onto our environment and what we create. Is this the best way to perceive or build? Maybe. After all, we are still, to date, the most successful species on the planet. Well, it depends on how you define success. I prefer to leave that to you.

With this introduction, I would like to hint to the reader that what you are about to read is human-written content about how we can morph artificial vision, created by us, into artificial touch, also created by us.
