# Journey

I think that one of the best achievements a human can have in his life is to leave a story worth reading. For people need different companions at different life instances, and sometimes a story of a life lived intentionally can be all you need.

This intro is to convince you to keep reading. I will be honest with you, the person who writes his story will alway need it more than those who read it. Thus I hope to inspire you with this to write your own.
